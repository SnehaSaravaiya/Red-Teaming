# Day 6: Prompt Injection

## What I Learned

- Prompt injection is when untrusted input influences an AI system's behavior or instructions.
- **Direct prompt injection** comes directly from the user.
- **Indirect prompt injection** can come from external content such as webpages, documents, or emails.
- AI systems can struggle to distinguish between trusted instructions and untrusted data.
- Prompt injection becomes especially important when AI systems have access to tools, private data, or external systems.

## Key Takeaway

> When an AI can read and act on external information, that information can become part of the security problem.

## Red Teaming Connection

A red teamer tests whether untrusted content can influence the model in unintended ways and documents the behavior, impact, and reproducibility.
