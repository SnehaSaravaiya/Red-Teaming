# Day 28: Comparative Model Evaluation

**Models:** GPT-5.4 mini and Grok 4.1 Fast

## Experiment
Compared both models using simple instruction-following tasks.

### Results
- **GPT-5.4 mini:** Followed the five-word constraint in both tests.
- **Grok 4.1 Fast:** Failed to meet the five-word constraint in both tests.
- Both models successfully completed the exact-output test.

## Key Observation
I expected both models to handle a simple word-count instruction correctly, but their results differed.

**Takeaway:** Even simple instructions should be tested rather than assumed to work reliably.

*Note: These results reflect only the tests performed, not overall model performance.*
